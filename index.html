How we choose

How each line is chosen and checked

01

Colour, all the way down the line

Shade is verified along the entire production line before a line goes on our list, so the same colour comes out of every batch — no drift between one order and the next.

02

Shaped like the real thing

We only list blooms whose petals and leaves follow the plant they copy, and we check samples in person before anything joins the collection.

03

Materials, dyes and components

We ask for material, dye and component testing on every line we list, so nothing harmful ends up in your venue or in your client's hands.

04

Packed for the journey

Each shipment is packed to protect heads and stems from bending and damage on the way to you, however far it travels.

The studio

One studio, one standard

Evonne Flora Co. began with one pair of hands and one stubborn idea: an artificial flower should hold up in a real venue — under warm lights, in close-up photos, at the back of a hall where nobody is looking at it.

Today the studio selects and supplies for wedding planners and event and exhibition decorators in several countries, and works the same way it always has: colour verified along the line, samples checked in person, packed properly for the journey.
