Sourcing Guide

Buying artificial flowers from China: a decorator's checklist

Sourcing artificial florals directly from a factory can cut cost and open up far more colours and styles. The key is confirming the right details before production starts.

Start with samples

Never place a bulk order from catalogue photos alone. Order samples of the exact flower types and colours you plan to use, then check them in the lighting and setting where they will appear.

Confirm colour matching in writing

Give a swatch, Pantone reference or photo and ask the supplier to confirm how colour will be matched and how reorders are kept consistent. This is the single most common source of disappointment in floral sourcing.

Be specific about quantity and packing

State the number of stems per colour, whether you need bunches or single stems, and how items should be packed. Good packing protects flower heads in transit and saves hours of reshaping on site.

Ask about custom options early

Custom stem length, bundling, private-label tags and packaging are often possible, but they need to be agreed before production. Tell the supplier your timeline so they can confirm what is realistic.

Plan shipping and documents

Confirm the destination market, required documents and preferred shipping method up front. Ask for a commercial invoice and packing list that match your goods, and clarify who handles import clearance.

Keep your specs on file

A reliable factory will archive your colours, packaging and product specs so reorders stay consistent. Ask whether they keep records — it saves a lot of back-and-forth on the second order.
