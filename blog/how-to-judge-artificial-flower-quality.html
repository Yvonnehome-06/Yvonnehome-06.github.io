Quality Guide

How to judge artificial flower quality before you order in bulk

A photo can make any flower look good. When you are buying hundreds of stems for a wedding or installation, the details decide whether the finished piece looks elegant or obviously artificial.

1. Look at the petals, not just the silhouette

Cheap flowers have flat, uniform petals with a plastic sheen. Better blooms have layered petals, subtle veining and slight colour variation between the edge and the centre. Ask for a close-up photo of a single head before you judge a whole range.

2. Check colour in daylight and under venue lighting

Warm indoor lighting and photography flash change how a colour reads. Request photos in natural light and, if the palette is critical, ask for a physical sample. Confirm that the supplier can batch-match colour so a reorder looks identical to your first order.

3. Feel the stem and check the assembly

A stem that bends too easily will droop in an arrangement. Check where the head meets the stem, how the wire is finished, and whether leaves are securely attached. These small things matter when stems are handled by a crew during setup.

4. Ask about material, not just "silk"

Many products marketed as silk are polyester or a blend. Different materials suit different budgets and looks — soft-touch finishes photograph beautifully, while standard plastic stems are practical for large outdoor installations. Ask what each item is actually made from.

5. Insist on pre-shipment photos

Ask for photos of the actual goods packed for your order, not catalogue images. A supplier confident in quality will always be happy to send them. At EVONNEHOME, pre-shipment photo reports are standard on bulk orders.
