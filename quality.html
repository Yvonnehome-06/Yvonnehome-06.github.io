Consistent colour, checked before shipping

For decorators, a flower that looks different from the sample is a real problem. We focus on the things that actually affect your install: colour, shape, material and packing.

FAQ

Common questions from decorators

Can you match a specific colour for our event?

Yes. Send a swatch, Pantone reference or photo and we'll select the closest match and confirm it with a sample before you place a bulk order. Batch-matched colour is standard.

Can you provide material or safety documents?

We provide material declarations with each order. If your market or venue requires specific safety testing, tell us the standard and destination and we'll arrange testing on request and confirm what is available before you order.

Will the flowers survive shipping and storage?

We pack to protect heads and stems in transit. On arrival, most items just need a light fluff and shaping. Care tips are included with your order.

Can you produce to our private label?

Yes. We can specify your labels, swing tags and packaging on the order, and keep your specs archived for fast, consistent reorders.
