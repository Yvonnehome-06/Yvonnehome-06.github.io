The studio

A one-woman floral studio, built around how your work actually looks

Evonne Flora Co. selects realistic silk and artificial flowers, greenery and ceremony accents for people who decorate for a living. It is not a catalogue of everything — every line is chosen for how it holds up on site, and checked the same way for every order.

  • Flowers, greenery and ceremony accents from one source
  • Sample first, bulk after — you approve it before we scale it
  • Colour verified along the whole production line
  • Your label, your packaging, and your spec kept for fast reorders

How we work

From your brief to the delivery

How we check every order
01

Share your brief

Send the flower types, colours, quantities and your event or product timeline. Photos, mood boards and venue shots are all welcome — the more context, the closer the first answer.

02

Sample & approve

We send physical samples, or a detailed photo set if that is easier for you, so you can check texture, colour and shape before committing to a bulk order.

03

Colour match

Batch-matched colour keeps every stem consistent — which is what makes a palette hold together across an entire venue instead of only in one photo.

04

Quality checks

Shape, colour and packing are checked before the goods leave, and you get video and photos of the actual batch for confirmation.

05

Shipping

Flexible shipping from major Chinese ports, with export documents prepared for your market and packing built to survive the journey.

06

Reorder fast

We archive your specs, colours and packaging, so a repeat order is quick and comes back looking exactly like the first one.
